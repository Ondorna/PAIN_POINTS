## Project Update

**Project:** Robotics project / “Ro-Mee-Bo (?)” therapeutic social robot

**Project purpose:**  
Build a therapeutic social robot for autistic children who are overwhelmed by stimulation. A therapist or instructor can use a limited vocabulary to drive and interact with the robot to engage a student with speech pathology. The broader effort includes developing an open-source robotics software stack, lowering cost, and giving the robotics group a kit to build, play with, and iterate on.

**Project lead:**  
Not explicitly stated in the notes. **Cory** is the presenter/contributor reporting on the project and says he joined it to bring the price down and is developing an open-source software stack.

**Current robot cost:**  
**$700**

**Target cost:**  
**$150**

**Technology stack:**  
**Open-source software stack for robotics.** The robot requires an **iPod Touch** in the back, which costs **$350**. The notes do not name specific languages, frameworks, or other technical components.

**Robotics group meeting day and time:**  
**Not specified in the meeting notes.**

**Distribution/manufacturing plan:**  
The project wants to **open source** the robot rather than sell it as a product. Interested manufacturers exist, and the robot/kit can be manufactured in a makerspace. The idea is to create a **kit of parts** that can be purchased, potentially sold at Noisebridge and manufactured there, with money going to Noisebridge for parts and materials. Trent suggested running workshops where people pay for a kit and build it with a knowledgeable person, with proceeds donated to Noisebridge.
