## Project Update: Ro-Mee-Bo (?) Therapeutic Social Robot

**Project purpose:**  
A therapeutic social robot for autistic children who are overwhelmed with stimulation. It lets a therapist or instructor using a limited vocabulary drive and interact with the robot to engage a student with speech pathology. The project aims to open source the robot and reduce its cost.

**Project lead:**  
Cory presented the project, said they recently joined the group, and are developing an open source software stack for robotics. The notes do not explicitly name a formal “project lead.”

**Current robot cost:**  
$700. About 100 are out in the wild. A major cost is the required iPod Touch in the back, which costs $350.

**Target cost:**  
$150.

**Technology stack:**  
Open source software stack for robotics. The current robot requires an iPod Touch in the back; the notes generally describe robots as “a pair of wheels, a battery, a brain, and a bunch of other stuff.”

**Robotics group meeting day and time:**  
Not specified in the meeting notes.

**Distribution/manufacturing plan:**  
The group does not want to sell products; they want to open source it. The idea is to make a kit of parts, with a kit that can be purchased. Manufacturers are interested in manufacturing things, and the robot can be manufactured in a makerspace. There is also discussion of making a package sold at Noisebridge, manufactured there, with money going to Noisebridge for parts and materials. Trent suggested a workshop route where people pay for the kit and work with someone knowledgeable to build it.
