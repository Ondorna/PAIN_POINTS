### Robotics Project Update

- **Purpose:** Develop an open-source, lower-cost robot for therapeutic interaction with autistic children, and create a reusable robotics kit for learning and iteration.
- **Project lead:** Cory.
- **Current cost:** About **$700** per robot.
- **Target cost:** **$150**.
- **Technology stack:** Node.js with a Blockly component.
- **Robotics group meeting:** The notes say the group meets to talk about robots “at the slightest opportunity,” but do not specify a day or time.
- **Distribution and manufacturing plan:** Develop a kit of parts that can be purchased and manufactured at Noisebridge, with interested manufacturers potentially producing components. One proposed route is to run workshops where participants build kits; sales proceeds could support Noisebridge’s parts and materials.
