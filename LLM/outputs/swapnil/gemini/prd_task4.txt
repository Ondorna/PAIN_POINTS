Based on the ConnectHub Product Requirements Document (Version 3.2), here is the concise V1 product scope summary:

*   **Approved V1 Features:**
    *   Direct Messaging System
    *   Group Channels
    *   User Profiles & Authentication
    *   File Sharing & Media Management
    *   Notification System
    *   User Authentication & Security
    *   Admin & Moderation Tools

*   **Features Under Review:**
    *   Video/Voice Calling (Engineering has proposed moving it to the Post-Launch phase due to timeline constraints, but the change has not yet received final approval).

*   **Is Video/Voice Calling planned for V1?**
    *   It was originally planned for V1, but its inclusion is currently under review following an engineering proposal to move it to the post-launch phase.

*   **Expected release phase for Video/Voice Calling:**
    *   Post-Launch (proposed by engineering, pending final approval).
