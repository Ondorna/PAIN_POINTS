**Project Update: Ro-Mee-Bo (name as recorded, with “(?)” in notes)**

- **Project purpose:** A therapeutic social robot for autistic children who are overwhelmed with stimulation. It lets a therapist or instructor with a limited vocabulary drive and interact with the robot to engage a student with speech pathology. The project also aims to reduce cost and open-source the robot, and give the Noisebridge robotics group a kit to build and iterate on.
- **Project lead:** Cory is the person presenting the project update in the meeting notes; no formal “project lead” title is stated.
- **Current robot cost:** $700 price point.
- **Target cost:** $150.
- **Technology stack:** Open-source software stack for robotics; robot described as a pair of wheels, a battery, a brain, and other parts. The current design requires an iPod Touch in the back, which costs $350.
- **Robotics group meeting day and time:** Not specified in the meeting notes.
- **Distribution/manufacturing plan:** The project wants to open-source the robot rather than sell products. The proposed plan is to create a kit of parts that can be purchased, with manufacturers interested in manufacturing things and the possibility of manufacturing in a makerspace. Cory asked how to make a package that can be sold at Noisebridge, manufactured there, with money going to Noisebridge for parts and materials. Trent suggested a workshop model where people pay for the kit and work with someone knowledgeable to build it, and/or put items in to sell with proceeds donated to Noisebridge.
