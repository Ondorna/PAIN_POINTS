Project purpose: A therapeutic social robot designed for autistic children who are overwhelmed with stimulation, allowing a therapist or instructor using a limited vocabulary to drive, interact, and engage the student who has a speech pathology.

Project lead: Cory

Current robot cost: $700

Target cost: $150

Technology stack: Open source software stack for robotics (the robot utilizes an iPod Touch in the back, and is structured as a pair of wheels, a battery, a brain, and other parts).

Robotics group meeting day and time: Not specified in the meeting notes.

Distribution/manufacturing plan: Manufacture the parts in a makerspace (Noisebridge) as a package/kit that can be sold, with all the money going to Noisebridge for parts and materials, and potentially holding workshops where people pay for the kit and work with someone knowledgeable to build it.
