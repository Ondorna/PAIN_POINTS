Project purpose: To serve as a therapeutic social robot for autistic children who are overwhelmed with stimulation, allowing a therapist or instructor using a limited vocabulary to drive, interact, and engage students with speech pathology.

Project lead: Cory

Current robot cost: $700 (partially due to requiring an iPod Touch in the back, which costs $350)

Target cost: $150

Technology stack: An open-source software stack for robotics being developed by Cory

Robotics group meeting day and time: Not specified in the meeting notes

Distribution/manufacturing plan: To create a kit of parts where parts can be purchased, manufactured in a makerspace (such as Noisebridge), and sold through workshops or at the space, with all the money going to Noisebridge for parts and materials.
