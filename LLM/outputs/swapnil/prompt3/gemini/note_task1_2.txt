**Project purpose:** To create a therapeutic social robot designed for autistic children who are overwhelmed with stimulation, allowing a therapist or instructor using a limited vocabulary to drive, interact, and engage students with speech pathology.

**Project lead:** Cory

**Current robot cost:** $700

**Target cost:** $150

**Technology stack:** An open-source software stack for robotics (utilizing a pair of wheels, a battery, a brain, and other standard parts; notably replacing the current iPod Touch requirement which costs $350).

**Robotics group meeting day and time:** Not provided in the meeting notes.

**Distribution/manufacturing plan:** Manufacture the robot parts in a makerspace (Noisebridge) as a kit of parts, potentially through holding workshops where participants pay for the kit and work with someone knowledgeable to build it, with proceeds donated to Noisebridge for parts and materials.
