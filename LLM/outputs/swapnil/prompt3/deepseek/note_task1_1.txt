| **Project purpose** | A therapeutic social robot for autistic children who are overwhelmed with stimulation. It allows a therapist or instructor using a limited vocabulary to drive and interact with it and engage a student who has a speech pathology. |
| **Project lead** | Not explicitly stated in the notes. **Cory** presented the project, said he recently joined the group, and is developing an open-source software stack for robotics. |
| **Current robot cost** | **$700** price point. |
| **Target cost** | Bring the price point down to **$150**. |
| **Technology stack** | Open-source software stack for robotics; hardware described as “a pair of wheels, a battery, a brain, and a bunch of other stuff.” The current design requires an **iPod Touch** in the back, which is cited as a major cost. |
| **Robotics group meeting day and time** | **Not specified in the meeting notes.** |
| **Distribution/manufacturing plan** | Open-source the project rather than sell or make products. Manufacturers are interested in manufacturing it, and it can be manufactured in a makerspace. Plan elements include developing a kit of parts, potentially selling a package at Noisebridge that is manufactured there with proceeds going to Noisebridge for parts and materials, and possibly using a workshop model where people pay for the kit and build it with someone knowledgeable. Trent also noted people can put things in the space to sell and have proceeds donated to Noisebridge. |
