The robotics group also wants a kit of parts to give people something to build and iterate on.

- **Technology stack:** Open-source software stack for robotics. No specific languages or frameworks are named. The notes describe robots generally as having wheels, a battery, a brain, and other parts; the current robot requires an iPod Touch.
- **Robotics group meeting day and time:** Not specified in the meeting notes.
