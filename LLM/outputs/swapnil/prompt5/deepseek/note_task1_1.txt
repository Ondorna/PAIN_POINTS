**Project Update: Ro-Mee-Bo (as transcribed; name marked “(?)” in notes)**

- **Project purpose:** A therapeutic social robot for autistic children who are overwhelmed with stimulation. It allows a therapist or instructor using a limited vocabulary to drive and interact with and engage the student who has speech pathology. The project also aims to give the robotics group a kit to start playing with and iterating on.
- **Project lead:** Cory presented the project and said he recently joined it; the meeting notes do not explicitly designate a project lead.
- **Current robot cost:** $700.
- **Target cost:** $150.
- **Technology stack:** An open-source software stack for robotics being developed by Cory. The current design requires an iPod Touch in the back, which costs $350. The robot is described generally as a pair of wheels, a battery, a brain, and other parts.
- **Robotics group meeting day and time:** Not provided in the meeting notes.
- **Distribution/manufacturing plan:** The project wants to open-source the robot rather than sell products. The idea is to make a purchasable kit of parts, with manufacturers interested in manufacturing things and the possibility of manufacturing in a makerspace. Cory asked how to make a package that can be sold at Noisebridge, manufactured there, with all money going to Noisebridge for parts and materials. Trent suggested workshops where people pay for the kit and build it with someone knowledgeable, with proceeds donated to Noisebridge.
