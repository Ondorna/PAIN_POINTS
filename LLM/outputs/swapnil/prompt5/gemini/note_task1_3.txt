Project update based on the meeting notes:

- **Project purpose:** To develop an open-source software stack and kit of parts for a therapeutic social robot designed to help autistic children who are overwhelmed with stimulation, allowing a therapist or instructor to drive, interact, and engage the student.
- **Project lead:** Cory
- **Current robot cost:** $700
- **Target cost:** $150
- **Technology stack:** An open-source software stack for robotics, powered by an iPod Touch in the back (though the project is working to reduce costs).
- **Robotics group meeting day and time:** Not specified in the meeting notes.
- **Distribution/manufacturing plan:** Manufacture the kit of parts at Noisebridge where people can build them, hold workshops where participants pay for the kit and work with someone knowledgeable to build it, and sell items with the proceeds donated to Noisebridge for parts and materials.
