Project purpose: A therapeutic social robot designed for autistic children who are overwhelmed with stimulation, allowing a therapist or instructor using a limited vocabulary to drive, interact, and engage students with speech pathology.

Project lead: Cory

Current robot cost: $700

Target cost: $150

Technology stack: An open-source software stack for robotics, powered by a pair of wheels, a battery, a brain, and an iPod Touch in the back.

Robotics group meeting day and time: Not specified in the meeting notes

Distribution/manufacturing plan: Manufacture the kit of parts at Noisebridge, potentially offering workshops where people pay for the kit and work with someone knowledgeable to build it, and selling items with the proceeds donated to Noisebridge for parts and materials.
