Based on the meeting notes provided, here is the project update:

* **Project Purpose:** To build a therapeutic social robot designed to help therapists or instructors engage and interact with autistic children who are overwhelmed with stimulation or have speech pathologies. 
* **Project Lead:** Cory
* **Current Robot Cost:** $700 (largely due to requiring a $350 iPod Touch in the back)
* **Target Cost:** $150
* **Technology Stack:** An open-source software stack for robotics (built around a robot with a pair of wheels, a battery, a brain, and other components).
* **Robotics Group Meeting Day and Time:** *Not mentioned in the notes.*
* **Distribution/Manufacturing Plan:** The creators want to open-source the project rather than sell products themselves. Potential plans include manufacturing the kit of parts in a makerspace, hosting workshops where people pay for the kit and build it with someone knowledgeable, selling items with the proceeds donated to Noisebridge, and working with interested manufacturers.
