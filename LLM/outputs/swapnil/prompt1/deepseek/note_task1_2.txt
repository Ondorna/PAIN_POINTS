## Project Update: Ro-Mee-Bo Robotics Project

**Project purpose:**  
A therapeutic social robot for autistic children who are overwhelmed with stimulation. It is designed so a therapist or instructor can use a limited vocabulary to drive and interact with the robot and engage a student with speech pathology. The project aims to lower the robot’s price point and open source it.

**Project lead:**  
Not explicitly identified in the meeting notes. **Cory** presented/described the project, said they recently joined the group, and is developing an open source software stack for robotics.

**Current robot cost:**  
$700 price point.

**Target cost:**  
$150.

**Technology stack:**  
An **open source software stack for robotics** is being developed. The notes describe robots generally as “a pair of wheels, a battery, a brain, and a bunch of other stuff,” and note that this robot currently requires an iPod Touch in the back, which is a major cost driver. Specific languages/frameworks are **not stated** in the notes.

**Robotics group meeting day and time:**  
**Not stated** in the meeting notes.

**Distribution/manufacturing plan:**  
The project wants to **open source** the robot rather than sell products. The plan involves creating a **kit of parts** that can be purchased, with manufacturers interested in manufacturing parts. The kit can also be manufactured in a makerspace. One proposed path is to sell a package at Noisebridge, manufactured there, with money going to Noisebridge for parts and materials. A workshop model was also suggested: people pay for the kit and build it with someone knowledgeable.
