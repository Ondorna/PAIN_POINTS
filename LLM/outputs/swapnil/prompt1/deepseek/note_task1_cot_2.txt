## Project Update

**Project:** Open-source therapeutic social robot, referred to in the notes as “Ro-Mee-Bo (?)”.

**Project purpose:** The project is developing a therapeutic social robot for autistic children who are overwhelmed with stimulation. A therapist or instructor with a limited vocabulary can drive and interact with the robot to engage a student with speech pathology.

**Project lead:** Cory presented and described involvement in the project. The meeting notes do not explicitly identify a project lead.

**Current robot cost:** $700.

**Target cost:** $150.

**Technology stack:** The project is developing an open source software stack for robotics. The robot is described as a kit-of-parts-style robot with wheels, a battery, a brain, and other components. The current design requires an iPod Touch in the back. Specific hardware or software technologies beyond that are not named in the notes.

**Robotics group meeting day and time:** Not specified in the meeting notes.

**Distribution/manufacturing plan:** The project aims to be open source rather than selling products. There are interested manufacturers, and the robot can be manufactured in a makerspace. The plan includes a kit of parts that can be purchased. Discussion included potentially selling a package at Noisebridge, manufactured there, with money going to Noisebridge for parts and materials. A workshop model was also discussed, where people pay for a kit and work with someone knowledgeable, with proceeds donated to Noisebridge.
