## 5. REFUND POLICY - OFFICIAL DOCUMENTATION

### Refund Eligibility & Timeline

**From Terms of Service (Last Updated: June 15, 2026):**

LearnHub offers a **14-day money-back guarantee** on all course purchases.

**Refund Details:**
- Refund window: 14 days from date of purchase
- Refund amount: 100% of course fee (minus transaction fees if applicable)
- Applicable to: Individual course purchases, bundle purchases, subscription first month
- Processing time: 5-10 business days

**Eligibility Criteria:**
- Purchase made within the last 14 days
- Course access time: Less than 30 hours
- No certificate of completion issued
- No enrolled in instructor's alternative course

**How to Request:**
- Log in to LearnHub account
- Navigate to "My Purchases"
- Select the course to refund
- Click "Request Refund" and provide reason
- Submit within 14-day window

### Refund Policy - Support FAQ (Last Updated: September 8, 2026)

**Common Questions About Refunds:**

**Q: How long do I have to request a refund?**  
A: You can request a refund within **7 days of your purchase**. This gives you a week to try out the course and make sure it meets your expectations.

**Q: What's the refund process?**  
A: It's simple. Go to "My Purchases," select the course, and click "Request Refund." We'll process it within 3-5 business days.

**Q: Am I eligible for a refund?**  
A: You're eligible if you meet these conditions:
- Purchased within the last 7 days
- Haven't completed the course
- Haven't earned your course certificate
- Haven't downloaded all course materials excessively

**Q: Do I get a full refund?**  
A: Yes, LearnHub offers a 100% refund of your course fee.

**Q: Can I get a refund after I've completed the course?**  
A: No. Once you've earned your certificate of completion, refunds are not available.

- Gifts cannot be refunded after redemption

**LearnHub Plus Members:**
- Cancel anytime, no penalties

- Access revoked immediately upon cancellation

### Promotions & Discount Codes

- Discounted courses follow same refund policy as full-price courses
- Refund amount: Original price paid (not original list price)
- Sales/promotion codes: Non-refundable once applied

- Contact enterprise@learnhub.com for details
