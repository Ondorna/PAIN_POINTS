## REFUND POLICY - SOURCE A: Terms of Service

**Document Date:** June 15, 2026  
**Document Type:** Official Terms of Service  
**Authority Level:** Platform Legal Document

### Refund Window

LearnHub offers a **14-day money-back guarantee** for all course purchases. If you are not satisfied with a course within 14 days of purchase, you may request a full refund with no questions asked.

**Details:**
- Refund period: 14 days from date of purchase
- Refund amount: 100% of course fee
- Process: Submit refund request through account settings
- Processing time: 5-10 business days after approval

### Eligibility Requirements

To qualify for a refund, you must meet ALL of the following conditions:
1. Request submitted within 14 days of purchase
2. Course access time less than 30 hours
3. No certificate of completion issued
4. Original payment method still valid

## REFUND POLICY - SOURCE B: Help Center FAQ

**Document Date:** September 8, 2026  
**Document Type:** Help Center Article  
**Authority Level:** Customer Support Documentation

### Can I get a refund for my course?

Yes, LearnHub allows refunds within **7 days of purchase**. This applies to individual courses purchased on our platform.

**Refund Details:**
- Refund window: 7 days from purchase date
- Full refund: Yes, 100% refund available
- How to request: Go to "My Purchases" > Select course > Click "Request Refund"
- Timeline: Refund processed within 3-5 business days

### Who is eligible for a refund?

You can request a refund if:
- You purchased within the last 7 days
- You have not completed the course
- You have not earned the course certificate
