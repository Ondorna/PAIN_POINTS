## CHRONOLOGICAL REFUND POLICY HISTORY

### Policy Version 1: Original Policy (Published January 10, 2026)

**Document Status:** Original Platform Policy  
**Effective Date:** Not explicitly stated  
**Last Updated:** January 10, 2026

---

**LEARNHUB REFUND POLICY - ORIGINAL**

LearnHub is committed to learner satisfaction. We offer a full refund for any course purchased on our platform if you request it within **7 days of purchase**.

**Refund Details:**
- Refund window: 7 days from purchase date
- Refund amount: 100% of course fee
- Processing time: 3-5 business days after approval
- Eligibility: Request within 7 days, no certificate earned

**How to Request:**
Contact our support team at support@learnhub.com with your course name and purchase confirmation.

---

### Policy Version 2: Interim Update (Published May 2, 2026)

**Document Status:** Platform Policy Update  
**Effective Date:** Not explicitly stated ("As of May 2, 2026")  

---

**LEARNHUB REFUND POLICY - INTERIM UPDATE**

Based on customer feedback, LearnHub has expanded its refund guarantee. We now offer **10 days** for refund requests to give learners more time to explore their courses.

**Updated Details:**
- Refund window: 10 days from purchase date (expanded from 7 days)
- Refund amount: 100% of course fee
- Processing time: 3-5 business days after approval
- Eligibility: Request within 10 days, less than 25 hours of access, no certificate issued

**Reason for Change:**
"Our analysis of learner feedback indicated that 7 days was not sufficient time for many students to determine if a course met their needs. We've extended this to 10 days while maintaining our quality standards."

---

### Policy Version 3: Current Policy (Published June 15, 2026)

**Document Status:** Current Official Policy  
**Effective Date:**"Effective June 15, 2026" 
**Replaces:** Not explicitly stated

---

**LEARNHUB REFUND POLICY - CURRENT VERSION**

LearnHub continues to enhance the learning experience. Our refund policy now reflects industry-leading standards with a **14-day money-back guarantee**.

**Current Details:**
- Refund window: 14 days from purchase date
- Refund amount: 100% of course fee
- Processing time: 5-10 business days after approval
- Eligibility: Request within 14 days, less than 30 hours of access, no certificate earned, no previous refunds on same course

**What's New:**
- Extended refund window from 10 days to 14 days
- Increased access time allowance from 25 hours to 30 hours
- Added clause about previous refund eligibility
