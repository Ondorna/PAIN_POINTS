## REFUND POLICY - DETAILED VERSION

### Comprehensive Refund Policy Overview

LearnHub is committed to customer satisfaction. We offer a 14-day money-back guarantee on all course purchases to ensure learners can assess whether a course meets their expectations before making a final commitment.

#### Refund Eligibility Window

**Primary Refund Period: 14 Days**

The refund window begins on the date of purchase and extends for 14 calendar days. For example:
- Purchase date: June 15, 2026 at 3:45 PM EST
- Refund deadline: June 29, 2026 at 11:59 PM EST

This 14-day period applies to all individual course purchases, course bundles, and the first month of LearnHub Plus subscriptions.

#### Eligibility Requirements (All Must Be Met)

**Requirement 1: Purchase Recency**
- Refund request must be submitted within 14 days of the original purchase date
- Purchase date is determined by the timestamp in your account payment history
- If you purchased on June 15, you have until June 29 to request a refund

**Requirement 2: Course Access Limitation**
- Total access time to course materials must be less than 30 hours
- This includes: video watching, quiz attempts, downloading materials, forum participation
- Platform tracks access time automatically in your account dashboard
- If you exceed 30 hours of access, you become ineligible for refund

**Requirement 3: No Certificate Issued**
- You must not have earned or been issued a Certificate of Completion
- Certificates are issued automatically upon course completion
- If a certificate has been issued, no refund is available under any circumstance
- Note: Even if you don't officially "complete" the course, if the certificate was earned, refund eligibility is lost

**Requirement 4: No Previous Refunds on Same Course**
- Each user is eligible for ONE refund per course
- If you previously refunded this course, you cannot request another refund
- This applies even if you repurchase the same course later

**Requirement 5: Valid Refund Request Channel**
- Refund request must be submitted through official LearnHub channels (account dashboard, email, or chat support)
- Informal requests (social media, comments, etc.) are not valid
- Request must include course name, purchase date, and reason for refund

#### Refund Processing Details

**Refund Amount:**
- 100% of the course fee paid
- Transaction fees may be deducted depending on payment method (typically $0-2)
- Promotional discounts already applied; refund is based on amount actually paid
- Currency of refund matches original payment currency

**Refund Processing Timeline:**
- Step 1: Submit refund request (instantaneous)
- Step 2: LearnHub reviews request (1-2 business days)
- Step 3: If approved, process refund (3-5 business days)
- Step 4: Refund appears in original payment account (bank/card dependent, typically 5-10 business days total)
- Total time: 5-10 business days average from submission to completion

**Exceptions to Processing Timeline:**
- Bank holidays may add 1-2 days
- International transfers may take longer (up to 15 business days)
- Debit card refunds typically faster than credit cards (3-5 days)

#### Non-Refundable Situations

The following purchases or circumstances are NOT eligible for refund under any circumstance:

1. **Bundles Partially Accessed:** If you've purchased a course bundle (3+ courses) and completed courses in the bundle, the entire bundle is ineligible for refund even if you only want to refund one course.

2. **Corporate/Enterprise Licenses:** Courses purchased through corporate accounts have different refund terms (see Enterprise section).

3. **Subscription Services:** LearnHub Plus subscription cancellations do not refund already-paid months; only prorated refunds for unused time apply.

4. **Gift Codes:** Refunds cannot be requested on gifts after the code has been redeemed. Only the gift purchaser can request a refund on an unredeemed gift code (within 30 days of purchase).

5. **Lifetime Access Courses:** Certain courses with "lifetime access" branding may have shorter refund windows (5 days); check course description.

6. **Certification Exams:** Exam fees are non-refundable once the exam has been started.

7. **Already-Completed Courses:** If you've completed the course and earned the certificate, even within 14 days, no refund is available.

#### How to Request a Refund

**Step-by-Step Process:**

1. Log into your LearnHub account
2. Navigate to "My Courses" → "My Purchases"
3. Find the course you wish to refund
4. Click the three-dot menu icon next to the course
5. Select "Request Refund"
6. Complete the refund request form:
   - Course name (auto-filled)
   - Purchase date (auto-filled)
   - Reason for refund (select from dropdown or write custom reason)
   - Confirm access hours (system shows current hours)
   - Accept terms and conditions
7. Submit the request
8. You'll receive a confirmation email within 1 hour
9. LearnHub support will review and respond within 1-2 business days

**Status Tracking:**
- After submission, you can track status in "Account" → "Refund Requests"
- Status updates: Submitted → Under Review → Approved/Denied → Processing → Completed

#### Refund Appeals & Disputes

If your refund request is denied, you can appeal:
- Submit appeal within 7 days of denial notice
- Provide additional information or dispute reasoning
- Appeals are reviewed by senior support team
- Final decision made within 3-5 business days
- If still denied, no further appeals available

Yes! LearnHub offers a **7-day refund guarantee** on all courses.

You have 7 days from purchase to request a refund. That's plenty of time to try out your course.

To get a refund, you need to:
- Request it within 7 days of purchase
- Not have spent more than 30 hours in the course
- Not have completed the course

1. Submit refund request in your account
2. We review it (usually takes 1-2 days)
3. If approved, you get 100% refund
4. Refund arrives in 5-10 days

Contact our support team at support@learnhub.com or use live chat.
