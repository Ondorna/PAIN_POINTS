## 5. REFUND POLICY (OFFICIAL)

### Core Refund Guarantee

**LearnHub's Commitment:** All learners have the right to assess whether a course meets their expectations before making a final commitment.

**The 14-Day Money-Back Guarantee:**

LearnHub offers a **14-day satisfaction guarantee** on all individual course purchases.

**Refund Window:**
- Period: 14 calendar days from date of purchase
- Start date: The timestamp in your account payment history
- End date: 11:59 PM EST on the 14th day
- Example: Purchase June 15 at 3:45 PM EST → Refund deadline June 29 at 11:59 PM EST

**Refund Amount:**
- 100% of course fee paid
- Transaction fees: Typically $0-2 (platform absorbs majority; some payment processors charge small fee)
- Promotional discounts: Refund based on actual amount paid (after discount)
- Special offers: Refund reflects discounted price paid
- Currency: Refund issued in same currency as original payment

**Processing Timeline:**
- Step 1: Submit refund request via account dashboard (immediate)
- Step 2: LearnHub review (1-2 business days)
- Step 3: Approval decision made (same timeframe)
- Step 4: Process refund to original payment source (instantaneous)
- Step 5: Funds appear in account (3-10 business days depending on payment method)
- Total average time: 5-10 business days from submission to receipt

**Processing Notes:**
- Bank holidays may add 1-2 business days
- International transfers may take up to 15 business days
- Debit card refunds: Typically 3-5 business days
- Credit card refunds: Typically 5-7 business days
- PayPal refunds: Typically 2-3 business days
- Status updates: Check "Account" → "Refund Requests" for real-time status

### Eligibility Requirements (All Must Be Met)

**Requirement 1: Within 14-Day Window**
- Refund request submitted within 14 calendar days of purchase
- Purchase date: Determined by timestamp in account payment history
- Verification: System automatically checks at submission
- No exceptions: Even 1 day late is outside window

**Requirement 2: Access Time Limit (Less Than 30 Hours)**
- Total course access time: Must be less than 30 hours
- What counts: Video watching, quiz attempts, material downloads, forum time, all activities
- What doesn't count: Account login without course access
- Tracking: Platform tracks automatically in real-time
- Verification: System shows total hours at refund request time
- No partial access: Even 30 hours and 1 minute exceeds limit

**Requirement 3: No Certificate Issued**
- Certificate of Completion status: Must NOT be earned
- Automatic issuance: Certificates issued automatically upon course completion
- Gray area: If you haven't "completed" course but certificate was earned, still ineligible
- Permanent: Once certificate issued, refund eligibility permanently lost
- Check status: Visible in your "My Certificates" dashboard

**Requirement 4: No Previous Refund on Same Course**
- Each user limited to ONE refund per course
- Covers both 1st purchase and any repurchases
- Permanent: If you previously refunded, cannot request again
- Applies to: Identical course, different course versions, or course updates

**Requirement 5: Valid Payment Account**
- Original payment method: Must still be valid/active
- Account status: No disputes, chargebacks, or fraud flags
- Refund destination: Must match original payment source
- Exceptions: For closed accounts, refund may be issued to registered email account

**Requirement 6: Account in Good Standing**
- No policy violations (academic integrity, harassment, spam)
- Account not restricted or suspended
- No pending investigations or disputes
- Terms of service accepted and followed

### How to Request a Refund

**Step-by-Step Process:**

1. **Log into LearnHub account**
   - Go to learnhub.com
   - Click "Sign In"
   - Enter credentials

2. **Navigate to Purchase History**
   - Click "My Account" (top right menu)
   - Select "My Purchases"
   - Locate the course to refund

3. **Initiate Refund Request**
   - Click three-dot menu icon next to course
   - Select "Request Refund"
   - System displays refund eligibility check

4. **Complete Refund Form**
   - Course name: Auto-filled (verify accuracy)
   - Purchase date: Auto-filled (verify accuracy)
   - Current access hours: Auto-filled (informational)
   - Certificate status: Auto-filled (informational)
   - Reason for refund: Select from dropdown OR write custom reason
   - Available reasons:
     * Course not matching expectations
     * Content too advanced or too basic
     * Better course found elsewhere
     * No longer need the skill
     * Technical issues with course
     * Other (please specify)
   - Comments: Optional text field (helpful for special circumstances)
   - Terms acknowledgment: Check box to confirm terms

5. **Review & Submit**
   - Review all information
   - Click "Submit Refund Request"
   - Confirmation message appears

6. **Receive Confirmation Email**
   - Sent within 1 hour of submission
   - Includes refund request ID
   - Confirmation of eligibility check
   - Timeline expectations

7. **Track Refund Status**
   - Go to "Account" → "Refund Requests"
   - Status updates in real-time:
     * "Submitted" (just sent)
     * "Under Review" (being evaluated)
     * "Approved" (ready to process)
     * "Processing" (funds being sent)
     * "Completed" (funds received)
   - Estimated completion date shown

### Refund Appeals & Disputes

**If Refund Request Is Denied:**

You have the right to appeal:
- **Timeline:** Submit appeal within 7 days of denial notice
- **Method:** Email support@learnhub.com with subject "Refund Appeal - [Refund Request ID]"
- **Required information:**
  * Your refund request ID
  * Reason for appeal (2-3 sentences)
  * Any supporting information
  * Screenshots if technical issue involved
- **Review process:** Appeals reviewed by senior support team member
- **Decision timeline:** Response within 3-5 business days
- **Final decision:** Cannot be appealed further; accepted as final

**Common Appeal Scenarios:**

**Scenario 1: "I was just outside the 14-day window"**
- If you submitted request on day 15 or later: Ineligible (window has passed)
- If system shows outside window but you believe you submitted on time: Appeal with email timestamp evidence

**Scenario 2: "My access hours are over 30 but I was still learning"**
- Platform counts all activities as "access"
- 30-hour limit applies regardless of learning depth
- Exceeding limit makes you ineligible
- No exceptions for "just barely over"

**Scenario 3: "I earned a certificate immediately after purchase"**
- Once certificate issued: Refund eligibility lost permanently
- No exceptions even if certificate issued within 14 days
- Certificate earned = course completed in system

**Scenario 4: "The course had technical problems"**
- Technical issues: Reason for appeal
- Document problem: Screenshots, error messages, etc.
- Possible outcomes: Refund approved or access to replacement course offered
- Support team will investigate

**Scenario 5: "I see conflicting information about the policy"**
- If multiple LearnHub documents show different policies: Appeal is valid
- Document the conflicting sources
- Explain which policy you relied on when purchasing
- Senior team will review and make determination

## 6. NON-REFUNDABLE ITEMS & EXCEPTIONS

### Situations Where Refunds Are NOT Available

**Bundle Purchases (Non-Refundable):**
- Skill Tracks: If you've completed any course in the bundle, entire bundle is non-refundable
- Career Paths: If you've accessed multiple courses, non-refundable
- Professional Certifications: Bundle purchase is non-refundable after first course started
- Partial bundles: Cannot refund individual courses from purchased bundle

**Subscription Services (Special Terms):**
- Monthly subscription: Cancelled immediately; no refund for current month
- Annual subscription: Prorated refund available (refund for unused days only, not full amount)
- Calculation: (Days remaining ÷ 365) × Annual price
- Example: Cancel after 100 days of $299/year → Refund = (265 ÷ 365) × $299 = $217
- Free trial period: No refund if within 7-day free trial

**Gift Purchases:**
- Unredeemed gift codes: Can be refunded (within 30 days of purchase)
- Redeemed gift codes: Cannot be refunded after code claimed
- Refund request: Must be submitted by original gift purchaser
- Alternative: Can request different course of equal value instead

**Corporate/Enterprise Purchases:**
- Custom terms: Subject to negotiated contract
- Minimum 100-seat purchase required
- Refund terms: Specified in enterprise agreement
- Contact: enterprise@learnhub.com for enterprise-specific refund policies

**Certification Exams (Non-Refundable):**
- Exam fees: Cannot be refunded once exam started
- Retake fees: Half price, available within 30 days
- Failing grade: No automatic refund; retake at half price

**Lifetime Access Premium Courses:**
- Marked as "Lifetime Premium" in course description
- Shorter refund window: 5 days instead of 14 days
- All other refund policies apply
- Clearly labeled at purchase time

**Completed Courses:**
- If you've earned certificate: Never refundable
- If completion is 100%: Never refundable
- If you've spent 30+ hours: Never refundable
- No exceptions under any circumstance

## 8. SPECIAL POLICIES & TERMS

### Subscription Cancellation

**LearnHub Plus Cancellation:**
- Anytime cancellation: No penalties, no lock-in contracts
- Immediate effect: Access revoked immediately upon cancellation
- Refund eligibility: Prorated for unused portion of subscription
- Reactivation: Can reactivate within 30 days without losing progress
- Progress retained: Course progress saved for 90 days after cancellation
- Certificates earned: Remain in your account permanently

**Refund Calculation for Annual Subscription:**
- Formula: (Days remaining ÷ 365) × Annual subscription cost
- Example: Cancel after 100 days of $299/year subscription
- Calculation: (265 days ÷ 365) × $299 = $217.17 refund
- Processing: 5-10 business days as with course refunds

### Promotions & Discount Codes

**Discount Code Application:**
- Codes apply to single purchase at checkout
- Cannot be stacked (one code per transaction)
- Refund calculation: Based on amount actually paid (after discount)
- Expiration: Codes have individual expiration dates shown at checkout
- Non-transferable: Cannot be transferred to other users

**Promotion Period Refunds:**
- Discounted courses: Follow same 14-day refund policy
- Price matching: If price drops within 7 days of purchase, claim price match refund
- Unused codes: Cannot be refunded; codes valid for duration specified
- Percentage discounts: Refund reflects discounted price paid

**Black Friday/Sale Refunds:**
- Sale courses: Same 14-day refund policy applies
- No exceptions for seasonal sales
- Price match guarantee: 7-day price match available

### Account Closure & Data Deletion

**Account Deletion Process:**
- Initiated via "Account Settings" → "Delete Account"
- 7-day grace period before permanent deletion
- Can cancel deletion within 7 days
- After 7 days: All data permanently deleted (unrecoverable)

**Upon Account Deletion:**
- Course access revoked immediately
- Certificates: Retained and can be viewed via public verification link
- Purchase history: Deleted
- Personal data: Deleted (GDPR compliant)
- Refund requests: Processed before account deletion if pending
