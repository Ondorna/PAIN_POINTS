Product Information — Metformin HCl Tablets, 500 mg
Indications: For the management of type 2 diabetes mellitus.
Dosage: Starting dose 500 mg twice daily with meals. Maximum 2000 mg/day.
Adverse Reactions: Diarrhea, nausea, abdominal discomfort, metallic taste.
